Our Achievements

More Than Awards.
A Culture of Excellence.

At Global International High School & Jr. College, achievement is viewed as a reflection of consistent effort, strong values, meaningful learning and the courage to aim higher.

From academic growth to sports, creativity, innovation and leadership, we encourage every learner to discover their strengths and transform potential into purposeful achievement.

Celebrating Every Dimension

Our Achievement Spectrum

Excellence takes many forms. We recognise the dedication, talent and growth that shape well-rounded individuals.

Academic Excellence

Encouraging strong academic foundations, curiosity, critical thinking and a lifelong commitment to learning.

Student Success

Celebrating student growth, confidence, consistency and the determination to pursue meaningful goals.

Sports Excellence

Promoting discipline, teamwork, resilience and the competitive spirit through sports and physical activities.

Arts & Creativity

Providing opportunities for students to express imagination, creativity and individuality beyond academics.

Innovation

Nurturing curiosity, problem-solving and innovative thinking to help students approach challenges differently.

Leadership

Developing responsible young leaders through collaboration, initiative, communication and service.

02 — DEVELOP
