About Our Institution

Empowering Students Through Excellence in Education

Global International High School & Jr. College is dedicated to providing quality education in a supportive and inspiring environment. We focus on academic excellence, holistic development, and preparing students for a successful future.

To empower students with knowledge, confidence, creativity, and strong values preparing them to achieve excellence and become responsible leaders of tomorrow.

Helpful Information

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers for parents and students. For anything else, please contact our school team.

1. How can I enquire about admission?

Use the Admission Inquiry form on this website or contact the school by phone or WhatsApp.

2. Which classes does the school offer?

Please contact the school office to confirm the classes and current admission availability for the academic year.

3. What documents are needed for admission?

Document requirements may vary by class. The school office will provide the latest checklist, which may include previous school records and identity documents.

4. How do I book a campus visit?

Call the school office before visiting so the team can guide you on a suitable time.

5. Where is the school located?

Open the Google Maps link in the Contact section for directions to Global International High School & Jr. College, Bhiwandi.

6. How can I contact the school?

Use the call, WhatsApp or email details shown in the Contact section of this website.

7. Where can I find school updates and activities?

Check the School Updates, Activities and Gallery sections for information published on the website.

8. How can I ask a question not listed here?

Send your question through the Admission Inquiry form or contact the school office directly.
