Our Educational Foundation

Learning With Purpose

At Global International High School & Jr. College, we believe that meaningful education develops more than academic knowledge. It nurtures curiosity, character, confidence and the ability to contribute positively to society.

Where We Aspire To Be

Our Vision

To nurture confident, knowledgeable and responsible individuals who possess the skills, values and vision to succeed in a changing world and contribute meaningfully to society.

What We Strive To Do

Our Mission

To provide quality and inclusive education through effective teaching, modern learning practices and meaningful opportunities that promote academic excellence, character, creativity, leadership and lifelong learning.

Our Educational Philosophy

Education Beyond The Classroom

We believe that education should prepare students not only for examinations, but for life. Every learning experience should help students understand, explore, create and contribute.
